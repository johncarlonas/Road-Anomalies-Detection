About

VISIRoad is a vision-based road anomaly detection system that helps identify dangerous road defects such as humps, cracks, and potholes. It enhances driver safety and infrastructure monitoring using real-time image processing — no AI or external sensors needed.

How to Use

  • Click Use in Road on the home page.
  • Allow camera access when prompted.
  • Hold your phone in landscape orientation and point it at the road ahead.
  • VISIRoad will automatically detect road anomalies and highlight them in real-time.
  • A timestamp and back button are available on the screen for easy navigation.
